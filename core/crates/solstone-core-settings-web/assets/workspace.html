checking…

your Granola meetings come into your journal at the times they happened, with their transcripts and notes. new and edited meetings follow within the hour.

how to get your key

API access comes with Granola Business and Enterprise. Granola Basic doesn't include it.

  1. open the Granola app on your computer.
  2. click your workspace name at the bottom left, then Settings.
  3. choose Connectors, then Personal API keys under API.
  4. click Create new key, tick Personal notes, and click Generate API Key.
  5. copy the key, which starts with grn_, and paste it below.

Personal notes covers the notes you own and the ones shared with you. on Granola Enterprise, an admin may need to allow API access first.

your journal keeps the key and uses it only to read your notes from Granola.

your meetings stay in your journal, and nothing comes in twice.
